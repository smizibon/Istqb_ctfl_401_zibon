1 · Why this page links instead of embedding

One honest paragraph on copyright.

The ISTQB publishes free sample exams for the CTFL certification — but those questions are copyrighted, and ISTQB's terms don't allow redistributing them inside third-party materials. Copying them into this guide isn't legal, and paraphrasing them would quietly change the wording the exam actually uses. So this guide does the honest thing: every mock, drill and walkthrough here is written from scratch (original questions only), and for the real official papers it links you straight to ISTQB. You get both — untainted original practice here, authentic official calibration there.

2 · Where to get them

Official sources — free downloads, no signup on the ISTQB site.

  • ISTQB — Certified Tester Foundation Level (CTFL) istqb.org/certifications/certified-tester-foundation-level The official CTFL page. The free sample exams (sets A–D) are downloadable from this page, along with the syllabus — look for the sample exam documents in the downloads/documents section.
  • ISTQB Glossary glossary.istqb.org The authoritative definitions of every exam term — the same source our glossary recall drill trains you on.

Many national boards host the same official samples on their own sites — e.g. ASTQB / AT*SQA (US), iSQI (Germany) and the BCS (UK). Same papers, same answers; use whichever board's site you'll actually sit the exam with, so the logistics you see are the logistics you'll get.

3 · How to use them with this guide

Official samples are the LAST step, not the first.

Take the official samples only as final dress rehearsals — after you've finished the three original mocks in this guide. If you sit them early you burn your only external check on material you're about to re-study. The calibration chain:

1 · Chapter quizzesscore ≥ 80% on every chapter quiz before moving on — gaps here are the cheapest to fix
→
2 · Original mocks 1–3score ≥ 26/40 (pass mark) on this guide's own mocks — fresh questions written for this syllabus
→
3 · Official samplessit them timed, closed-book as the external check — the closest thing to exam day

Chapter quizzes ≥ 80% → original mocks ≥ 26/40 → official samples as the external check. Below 26/40 on an official sample: go back to the weakest chapter, not to another mock.
