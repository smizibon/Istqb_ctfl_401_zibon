What is this? ISTQB weights every question by cognitive level: K1 recall — recognise terms, lists, definitions (~20% · ~7–8 of 40); K2 understand — scenarios, comparisons, classification (~55% · ~21–23); K3 apply — derive the answer by hand (~25% · ~10–11). The pass is built on K1+K2; K3 is the practisable margin.
